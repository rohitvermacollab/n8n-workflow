## 📧 AI Email Assistant

Automatically reads incoming emails and prepares smart replies — so you never miss an important message.

**What it does:**
1. Watches your inbox for new emails
2. Uses Gemini AI to understand and classify each email
3. Logs every email to a Google Sheet for tracking
4. Writes a reply with AI for emails that need one
5. Saves the reply as a Gmail draft — you just review and hit send

**Tools used:** n8n, Google Gemini, Google Sheets, Gmail

**Saves time on:** customer support, sales inquiries, busy inboxes
